Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

Does SOV choose the artist?

No. Your panel or commission decides. We run eligibility, organize the panel and document the decision. AI never selects or rejects artists.

Can artists apply without using the website?

Yes. We can accept applications by email, by phone or with assistance, so no eligible artist is shut out by technology.

Can we export our program data?

Always. Your rosters, applications and records stay yours and export at any time.
