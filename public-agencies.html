Get started
  1. Program design
    Rules you set

    Eligibility, budget, schedule and selection criteria, decided by you.

  2. Call for artists
    RFQ or RFP

    We write it with you, publish it to our network and yours, and support applicants. Artists never pay to apply.

  3. Review
    Eligibility and panel

    Eligibility screening, panel workspace, rubrics and conflict-of-interest disclosures.

  4. Selection
    Your decision

    Your panel or commission approves the artist. We document the decision.

  5. Engagement
    Community input

    Community sessions and design refinement with the selected artist.

  6. Delivery
    Permits, fabrication and install

    Right-of-way and building permits, engineering, fabrication and install, with milestone approvals.

  7. Dedication and after
    Reporting and care

    Dedication, reporting, a maintenance plan, and a roster ready for the next call.

Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

Does SOV choose the artist?

No. Your panel or commission decides. We run eligibility, organize the panel and document the decision. AI never selects or rejects artists.

Can artists apply without using the website?

Yes. We can accept applications by email, by phone or with assistance, so no eligible artist is shut out by technology.

Can we export our program data?

Always. Your rosters, applications and records stay yours and export at any time.

Rather talk it through first?

Not ready for a brief? Start with a conversation. A person on our team writes back, and there is no obligation.
