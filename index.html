What SOV carries

We hold the risk neither side can see.

Procurement needs clear costs, insurance and paperwork. Creatives need fair pay and protection. Most deals fall apart in the gap. That gap is our job.

  • Vetted once, by people who do the work. A video call, references and a delivery record.
  • Vendor setup done for you. Insurance certificate, W-9 and contract ready before anyone climbs a lift.
  • Labor and materials budgeted apart. A price change in paint or wood never eats the creative's pay.
  • Money held, then released by milestone. You approve, then it moves.
  • Rights in writing. Buying a work never quietly hands over the copyright.

Fees

One fee, on its own line.

You see where every dollar goes before you sign. Our fee is 10% when we find your creative and 15% when we run the whole project, always inside your budget. Creatives never pay to be booked.
