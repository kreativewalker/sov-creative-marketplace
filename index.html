Our story

SOV started on the other side of a finished wall.

Our founder spent fourteen years selling, delivering and getting paid for murals, placements and cultural programs. Most of what made it hard happened around the work: the scope nobody wrote down, the invoice he had to chase, the client who wasn't sure who to trust. SOV is what that workaround became once it had to hold more than one person.

What SOV carries for you

Your team needs clear costs, insurance and paperwork. Independent artists and designers need fair pay and protection. Most projects stall in the space between, and that space is our job.

  • Vetted once, by people who do the work. A video call, references and a delivery record.
  • Vendor setup done for you. Insurance certificate, W-9 and contract ready before anyone climbs a lift.
  • Labor and materials budgeted apart. A price change in paint or wood never eats the creative's pay.
  • Payments tied to documented approvals. Submission, approval and payment are separate steps, so nothing gets paid by accident or held up by a lost email.
  • Codes, permits and historic review planned in. We flag what applies early and build it into the schedule and budget.
  • Rights in writing. Buying a work never quietly hands over the copyright.

How we protect quality

Every project follows the same standard, whether it's a single portrait commission or a year of programming.

A rubric you can read

Every Registry artist and designer is vetted on commercial readiness, technical skill and cultural grounding. We share the rubric with you before you choose.

A cultural alignment brief

You and the creative both sign off on the brief before work starts, so the voice of your brand or mission is clear from day one.

Reviewed before you see it

An SOV project lead checks each milestone against the brief before it reaches your desk.

Paid in three stages

40 percent to start, 40 percent at the midpoint and 20 percent at handoff, each released only after your approval.

A replacement if life happens

If a Registry creative can't finish because of an emergency, we bring in another of equal caliber to keep your deadline.

A 14-day technical warranty

For digital, written and audio work, we fix broken files, typos and dropouts for 14 days after handoff at no cost.

One price, every line visible

Every project is priced on its scope and complexity, not a flat percentage. You see one total investment, then exactly what it covers, before you sign. Artists and designers never pay to be booked.
