Public art residents claim as theirs.

Cities, counties, transit, parks and downtown districts use public art to tell local stories, bring people back to a place, and mark a moment. We run the parts that usually slow it down.

The pilot

One public artwork, or one community design process

We can write and run your call for artists (RFQ), support your selection panel, lead community engagement, and keep the public record your council needs.

Many cities fund public art through a percent-for-art program, often around 1 percent of eligible capital project budgets. We'll work inside your rules.

What you're worried about
  • A procurement process that drags on
  • Community pushback after it goes up
  • Maintenance after the ribbon cutting
What we handle
  • Calls for artists, panel review and contracts
  • Community input sessions and design review
  • Insurance, permits, engineering and install
What you get
  • A work residents are proud of
  • A clear record of every decision
  • A maintenance plan and conservation notes
