How pricing works

Priced on your project, not a percentage.

A small mural and a multi-site program are different jobs. We price each project on its scope and complexity: how many sites, creatives and approvals, what has to be fabricated, shipped and installed, and how fast. You see the total, then every line, before you sign.

At the top of every proposal

Total project investment

  • Approved scope and key deliverables
  • Production timeline and review rounds
  • Who installs, and when
  • Payment schedule
  • Rights included
Then, line by line
Creative servicesThe artist, designer, performer or creative team's pay
ProductionMaterials, fabrication, equipment and specialist labor
LogisticsPacking, freight, travel, install and removal
Project requirementsInsurance, permits, engineering, historic or code review
Project deliverySOV curation, project management, documentation and coordination

Planning a budget? Many developers set aside about 0.5 to 1 percent of construction cost for art, and many public programs use a percent-for-art rule of around 1 percent of eligible capital budgets. We'll help you size yours in the first conversation.
