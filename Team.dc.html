Where AI helps, and where it stops

Green: AI can do it. Summaries, reminders, internal tags, draft checklists.

Yellow: AI drafts, a person approves. Match rankings, briefs, proposals, outreach.

Red: people decide. Contracts, prices, payments, turning an artist down, anything public.

Our AI concierge gives guidance, not legal, tax or insurance advice. A person checks every decision about money or contracts, and you can always ask to talk to someone.
