Questions artists and designers ask

What does it cost?

Applying to the Registry and being booked is free, with no placement fee. Clients pay SOV's fee. Studio, Studio+ and Advisory are optional memberships.

What if I'm not accepted?

Join Studio for templates, the wellness hub and community. Studio+ adds feedback on your application.

I'm not in North Carolina. Can I apply?

Yes. We're piloting in North Carolina and matching as work opens up where you are or where you'll travel.

Do I need my own insurance?

Not to apply. When a project requires coverage, we coordinate it with you before work starts.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Clients buy the piece or a license, and every use is written into the agreement.

Does joining guarantee work?

No. Registry members are matched when a project fits, and you can always say no.
