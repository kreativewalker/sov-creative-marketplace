Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

What if the performer cancels?

Every booking has a backup creative identified in advance, and your SOV contact handles the swap.

Can we use the photos and video?

Yes, within the rights written into the agreement. We agree photo, video and social use before the event.

Our venue is union. Is that a problem?

No. Tell us in the brief and we plan around house rules and jurisdiction from the start.

Rather talk it through first?

Not ready for a brief? Start with a conversation. A person on our team writes back, and there is no obligation.
