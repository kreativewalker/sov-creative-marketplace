About SOV

Built by people who have done the work.

SOV is a creative marketplace run like a studio. We turn what an organization wants a space to feel like into a clear brief, find the right creatives, deliver the work, and protect the originality that makes it worth having.

We started in Greensboro because we've stood on both sides of the deal: the creative chasing an invoice, and the business unsure who to trust. We're piloting in North Carolina, and building the platform to work in any city or country.

How we measure ourselves

Not clicks or sign-ups. Finished work, paid fairly.

Our one number is paid creative projects completed well for both sides: the organization that commissioned the work and the creative who made it.

Every year, in public

The SOV Creative Economy Report

What was spent, what it was spent on, and how much reached creatives, broken down by type of work. Shown as totals, medians and ranges only, and never published for a category small enough to identify anyone.

North Carolina's nonprofit arts and culture sector alone drives about $2.23 billion in yearly economic activity. We want the share that reaches the people making the work to be visible too.

Source: Arts and Economic Prosperity 6, North Carolina Arts Council.

The name

Short for sovereignty, built in community.

The name is the promise. Businesses keep a clear scope, budget and final say. Creatives keep their pay, their rights and their name. We hold the deal in between, and we believe the work gets better when people also gather in the same room.
