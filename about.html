About SOV

Built by people who have done the work.

SOV is a creative marketplace run like a studio. We turn what an organization wants a space to feel like into a clear brief, find the right creatives, deliver the work, and protect the originality that makes it worth having.

We started in Greensboro because we've stood on both sides of the deal: the creative chasing an invoice, and the business unsure who to trust.
