Questions about pricing

Plain answers, before you start.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Start free for 30 days. We'll ask you to choose a plan before the trial ends, and nothing is charged until you do.

What's the difference between a plan and a project fee?

A plan is the software: posting, project rooms, approvals, payments and records. If you want SOV to find the creative and run the project, that's a curated match, priced per project.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. Move up or down any time. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the month. Annual plans include two months free.

Do creatives ever pay to be booked?

Never. Creatives join, apply and get booked free. Studio is optional and has no effect on invitations.

Do you offer pricing for nonprofits and arts councils?

Yes. Talk with us about pilot and annual agreements.
