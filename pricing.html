Questions about pricing

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Start free for 30 days. Nothing is charged until you choose a plan.

What's the difference between a plan and a project fee?

A plan is the software. A curated match, where SOV finds the creative and runs the project, is priced per project.

Can I switch plans or cancel?

Yes. Move up or down any time. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the month.

Do artists and designers ever pay to be booked?

Never. Memberships are optional and have no effect on invitations.

Is there pricing for nonprofits and cultural institutions?

Yes. Talk with us about pilot and annual agreements.
