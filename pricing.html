Three ways to work

Start with one and move up any time. Every tier has vetted creatives and line-by-line pricing.

Post an opportunity

Self-serve. Post the project, review who applies and choose, or invite someone you already know. SOV gives you agreements, payments and a project room.

Curated match

Full service. Share your goals, budget and timing. SOV writes the brief, shortlists 3 to 5 creatives and runs agreements, insurance, install and payment with one lead.

Creative program

Year-round or across several sites. You set the standards. SOV plans the year, keeps a preferred roster and reports across every site.

Questions about pricing

Is it really free?

Yes, while we're in beta. Make a free account and use the full dashboard. We'll tell you well before anything costs money, and nothing is ever charged without your say.

What's the difference between a plan and a project fee?

A plan is the dashboard and platform. A curated match, where SOV finds the creative and runs the project, is quoted per project with every line visible.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Move between Property, Team and Portfolio any time.

Do artists and designers ever pay to be booked?

Never. Their memberships are optional and never affect who gets invited.

Is there anything special for nonprofits and cultural institutions?

Yes. Talk with us about pilot and grant-friendly agreements.
