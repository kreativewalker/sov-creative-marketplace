What's on your plate

Opening dates don't move.

Art and programming are often the last line on the punch list, and the first thing guests notice.

A fixed opening date

Pre-opening schedules leave no room for a commission that arrives late or a creative nobody can reach.

Brand standards

Work has to fit the flag, the design intent and the FF&E package, and still get ownership to say yes.

Guests on property

Renovations happen while you are open. Installs can't block the lobby or wake a floor.

Costs after approval

A price that grows after the PO, through freight, framing or "extras," breaks the budget.

Maintenance

Housekeeping and engineering have to live with the work for years.

Generic art

Pieces that could hang in any hotel, anywhere, and tell guests nothing about where they are.

Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

We already have a designer, and maybe an art consultant.

Good. We work alongside your design team. We can source local creatives for the pieces they specify, handle delivery for a creative they found, or run the whole program.

Can you do guest rooms at scale?

Yes. Most properties use originals in public areas and licensed editions in rooms. We source both and write the rights into the agreement.

Will the install disturb guests?

We plan installs around occupancy, in after-hours windows agreed with your engineering team, and we leave the space clean.
