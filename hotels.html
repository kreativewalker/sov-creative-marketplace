Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

We already have a designer, and maybe an art consultant.

Good. We work alongside your design team. We can source local creatives for the pieces they specify, handle delivery for a creative they found, or run the whole program.

Can you do guest rooms at scale?

Yes. Most properties use originals in public areas and licensed editions in rooms. We source both and write the rights into the agreement.

Will the install disturb guests?

We plan installs around occupancy, in after-hours windows agreed with your engineering team, and we leave the space clean.
