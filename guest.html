Two sides of one deal

A hotel wants a lobby guests remember. A muralist wants to be paid on time and keep the rights. Both want the same project to go well. SOV carries the scope, agreements, approvals and payments in between, so neither side has to.

Experiential brands get

Vetted artists and designers who know their community. One price, every line visible. A dashboard for every step, and nothing paid until they approve it.

Artists and designers get

Their full pay shown before they say yes. Rights kept and licensed. A studio dashboard for every invitation, milestone and payout, plus wellness and a community.
