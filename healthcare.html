What's on your plate

The work has to be safe before it can be beautiful.

Every piece passes through infection prevention, facilities, patient experience and procurement. Most creatives have never navigated that.

Inappropriate imagery

Work that unsettles a patient population, or misses the people it is for.

Infection control

Porous materials, hard-to-clean surfaces or an install that breaks containment.

Maintenance

Work environmental services can't clean, or that wears badly in a high-traffic corridor.

Stakeholder approval

A project that stalls because patients, staff or leadership were not part of it.

Vendor documentation

Credentialing, insurance and paperwork that arrive incomplete.

Representation

A community that doesn't see itself in the spaces where it receives care.

    Cleaning and care instructions, documentation for your environment-of-care records, and optional programs.

Questions we hear

Before you reach out.

More answers on how it works.

What imagery works in healthcare?

It depends on who the space serves. Nature, calm color and local places are common starting points. We build imagery guidance with your team before any concept is made.

Can a foundation or donor fund the project?

Yes. We can scope donor-funded commissions, follow your recognition rules and report back to the foundation.

Do your creatives meet our vendor credentialing?

We handle the vendor documentation, and creatives complete your credentialing before any on-site work.
