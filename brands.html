What SOV carries for you

You shouldn't need a procurement department to commission a mural. Projects stall between the creative and your finance, legal and facilities teams. We fill that gap.

Vetting
Every creative met on video, references checked, past work delivered.
Vendor setup
Certificate of insurance, W-9 and contract in hand before anyone starts.
Budgets
Materials budgeted apart from the creative's fee, so surprises don't hit your budget or their pay.
Codes and permits
Historic review, permits and ADA flagged before they cost you an opening date.
Rights
You know exactly where, how and how long you can use the work.
