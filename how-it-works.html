Three ways to work

Start with one and move up any time. Every tier: vetted creatives, line-by-line pricing, and creatives never pay to take part.

Post an opportunity

Self-serve. Post the project, review who applies and choose, or invite someone you already know. SOV gives you the network, agreements, payments and a project room.

Curated match

Full service. Share your goals, budget and timing. SOV writes the brief, shortlists 3 to 5 creatives and runs agreements, insurance, install and payment with one lead.

Creative program

Year-round or across several sites. You set the standards. SOV plans the year, keeps a preferred roster and reports across every site.

Questions

Do I need a big budget?

No. Projects are priced on scope. A first engagement can be a single commission.

Can I bring a creative I already know?

Yes. Invite them into a self-serve project and SOV still handles agreements and payments.

Are you only in North Carolina?

We're piloting in North Carolina, and the platform is built to work in any city or country.

Rather talk it through first?

Twenty minutes on your project, your goals and your timing. A person on our team writes back, with no obligation.
