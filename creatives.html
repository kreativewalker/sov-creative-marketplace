Applying to the SOV Registry

The Registry is where commercial work comes from. Applying is free and starts the vetting process. If you aren't accepted this round, Studio is there to help you grow, and Studio+ includes feedback on your application.

  1. 1Send your application.
    Your CV, your bio, your artist or design statement, and five pieces that show your strongest work.
  2. 2Have a video call.
    About 30 minutes on Google Meet with a working creative from our team. A conversation, not a test.
  3. 3Set your offers.
    Commissions, sales, art rental, image licensing, design, activations and teaching. Your rates.
  4. 4Get matched.
    We actively match you with clients whose projects fit your work, with the budget, your fee and the rights shown up front.
  5. 5Stay current.
    Registry status is reviewed once a year, so clients know every member is active and delivering.

Questions artists and designers ask

I'm a musician or performer. Is this for me?

Yes. Hotels, cultural institutions and event hosts book live music, dance, poetry and performance for openings and programs. You can share video or audio links instead of photos.

I'm not in North Carolina. Can I apply?

Yes. We're testing projects in North Carolina first, but anyone can apply from anywhere. We're building toward projects across the US and internationally, and we'll match you as work opens up near you or where you're willing to travel.

Do I need my own insurance?

Not to apply. Many creatives don't carry it all year. When a project requires coverage, we coordinate it with you before work starts.

Do I need a big portfolio or a gallery history?

No. Five strong pieces, your CV, bio and statement, and a clear sense of how you work are enough to start the conversation. Garage studios welcome.

What does it cost?

Applying to the Registry and being booked costs nothing, and there is no placement fee. Clients pay SOV's fee, and your agreed pay is shown in full before you accept. Studio, Studio+ and Advisory are optional memberships for growing your practice.

What if I'm not accepted into the Registry?

You can still join Studio for the templates, wellness hub and community, and Studio+ adds group office hours and feedback on your application so you can apply again stronger.

Does joining guarantee me work?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. Approval means you can be invited when a project fits. We bring invitations, never bidding wars, and you can always say no.

Do you use AI on my work?

Only to help our team sort and describe, never to choose or reject you. People make every decision. We never train AI on your work without your written permission, and we ask you to tell us when AI is part of your own process.

Who owns what I make?

You do. A client buys the piece or a license to use it, and every use is written into the agreement. Anything beyond that, like merch or ads, is a separate license and a separate payment.

Can I still work with my own clients?

Yes. SOV isn't exclusive. Keep working with the people you already know.

Who sees my information?

Only you and our review team, until you're approved and say yes to a public profile. We never sell your information.
