Our story

Everyone who shapes culture deserves to be paid, protected and seen.

The issue

Creative work drives how places feel and how missions are remembered. The system around it still leaves the people who make it behind.

Where it breaks

Organizations become project managers.

Briefs, contracts, insurance and payments land on teams with no time for them.

Creatives work for free first.

Unpaid proposals, handshake deals and late invoices decide who can afford to stay.

The record disappears.

Rights, files and credit scatter across inboxes, and the work loses its history.

Our answer is the system in between.

SOV carries the scope, agreements, approvals, payments and records that let culturally grounded work happen again and again.

Where it began

SOV started in Greensboro, North Carolina, with an artist who spent fourteen years on both sides of the deal: the creative chasing an invoice, and the client unsure who to trust. Placing other artists' work showed the same gaps from the other side of the table.

Before building, we studied 315 pages of research citing 2,668 sources. Every question in our brief and every rule in our agreements traces back to it.

SOV is short for sovereignty.

The name is the promise. Organizations keep a clear scope, budget and final say. Artists and designers keep their pay, their rights and their name. We hold the deal in between, and we believe the work gets better when people also gather in the same room.
