Paid, protected and seen.

Everyone who shapes culture deserves all three. This is why most don't get them yet, and what SOV is building so they can.

The issue

Arts and culture added $1.17 trillion to the US economy in 2023, 4.2% of GDP. It shapes how places feel and how missions are remembered. The system around it still leaves the people who make it behind.

$20–30KMedian yearly income reported by visual artists. Almost half said their art brings in 10% of it or less.

Where it breaks

Organizations become project managers.

Briefs, contracts, insurance and payments land on teams with no time for them.

Creatives work for free first.

Unpaid proposals, handshake deals and late invoices decide who can afford to stay.

The record disappears.

Rights, files and credit scatter across inboxes, and the work loses its history.

Our answer is the system in between.

SOV carries the scope, agreements, approvals, payments and records that let culturally grounded work happen again and again.
